Find answers and learn how SparkSocial works

⚡ Getting Started

What is SparkSocial?

SparkSocial is a social ecosystem where people can connect, share content, communicate, build communities, discover opportunities, and grow together.

How do I create an account?

Create an account using the signup page, complete your profile, and start connecting with the SparkSocial community.

📱 Social Features

How do I create a post?

Go to the Home feed, write your content, then tap the Share Post button.

How do I follow someone?

Visit Discover, find a user, and tap Follow to connect with them.

How do messages work?

Open Messages, select a user, and start a conversation instantly.

🚀 Creator & Community

What can creators do on SparkSocial?

Creators can share content, build audiences, connect with communities, and unlock future creator tools.

What are communities?

Communities allow people with shared interests to connect, communicate, and grow together.

🪙 Rewards & Future Features

What are Spark Coins?

Spark Coins are part of the SparkSocial reward ecosystem used for future platform experiences.

What is XP?

XP represents your activity and progression inside the SparkSocial ecosystem.

🔒 Safety

How does SparkSocial protect users?

SparkSocial is designed with privacy, security, and positive community experiences in mind.

How do I report a problem?

Use the Report Center or contact support if you experience issues.

💙 Still Need Help?

Our support team is here to help you with questions, feedback, and problems.
