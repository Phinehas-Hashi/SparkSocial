🔒 SparkSocial Privacy Policy

How we protect and manage your information

⚡ Our Privacy Promise

At SparkSocial, we believe trust is the foundation of every community. We are committed to protecting your information and providing a safe environment where people can connect, create, and share.

👤 Information We Collect

SparkSocial may collect information needed to provide platform features such as:

• Profile information
• Posts and interactions
• Messages and communication features
• Account activity
• Preferences and settings

🚀 How We Use Information

Information helps us provide, improve, and personalize your SparkSocial experience. We use information to:

• Provide platform features
• Improve user experience
• Maintain security
• Develop new features

💬 Social Interactions

When you share content, communicate, or interact with others, your activity may be visible according to your privacy settings. You control what you choose to share.

🔒 Account Security

We work to protect accounts and user information through security practices designed to keep SparkSocial safe. Users should also protect their login information.

⚙️ Your Controls

You can manage your information through your SparkSocial settings. You may update profile details, control interactions, and manage your preferences.

🌍 Future Platform Features

As SparkSocial grows, new features such as creator tools, communities, rewards, and other experiences may introduce additional privacy considerations. We will continue improving transparency.

🤝 Community Safety

We work to maintain a respectful environment by encouraging positive interactions and addressing harmful behavior.
